How to get sprites and variants
  • Tear down a Wanted Poster and it turns into a Sprite Treasure Map. Follow it to a Buried Sprite Chest and dig it up with your pickaxe.
  • Sprites also drop from chests, Ruby Sprite Chests, and players carrying one.
  • Fortune (ruby red): keeps the base power and reveals nearby chests. Each one is tied to a named location.
  • Cursed: better odds at rare variants, but water hurts you. In the files but not live yet.
  • Gold sprites return Nov 12 (bonus XP for eliminations). Not on here until the list is out.
  • Mastery rewards are under the Treasure tab. 40 mastered unlocks the Navigator's Nosedive Umbrella.
